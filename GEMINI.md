## Quick Rules Summary
1. **Domain & DTO Purity:** Files in `lib/domain/` and `lib/infrastructure/dtos/` MUST NOT import `package:flutter/...`. Use pure Dart annotations (`package:meta/meta.dart`). Verified by `domain_purity_test.dart` and `dto_purity_test.dart`.
2. **Ports & Adapters:** Interfaces go in `lib/domain/ports/`; implementations go in `lib/infrastructure/repositories/`.
3. **CvRDT Immutability & Safety:** `HybridLogicalClock`, `CrdtLwwRegister`, `CrdtOrSet`, and minion summons (`AnimatedObjectInstance`) mutations return new immutable instances. Internal items and tombstones in `CrdtOrSet` are sealed using `Map.unmodifiable()` across constructors (`CrdtOrSet`, `const CrdtOrSet.empty()`) and operations (`addBatch`, `remove`, `merge`, `pruneTombstones`). Eradicate all silent empty setters and deprecated mutating methods (`takeDamage`, `heal`, `applyHeal`, `grantTempHp`), enforcing pure copy-transforms (`applyDamage`, `applyHealing`, `applyTempHp`). Use cryptographically secure pure UUID v4 node identifiers for deterministic tie-breakers (e.g. `HomebrewImportOrchestrator`), implement sliding lookback deduplication windows (`inboundTimestamp >= localTime - 30000`) with bounded O(1) amortized `LinkedHashMap<String, bool>` SHA-256 caching (500 entries) in `CascadingTransportRouter` and `RoomSyncOrchestrator`, require `networkTimeProvider` constructor injection in `RoomStateReconciliationService`, synchronize inbound network synchronization under `_syncMutex.protect()`, offload cold storage JSON encode/decode to `Isolate.run()` in `StorageDurabilityCoordinator`, and implement `prune(threshold)`.
4. **DTO Safety:** Map JSON to DTOs in `lib/infrastructure/dtos/` with numeric bounds clamping and `unparsedPayload` retention.
5. **Asynchronous Stream Emission:** Broadcast StreamControllers in repositories and P2P transport adapters (`LocalCampaignRepository`, `CascadingTransportRouter`, `RoomSyncOrchestrator`, `FirebaseFallbackAdapter`, `FirebaseSignalingAdapter`, `LocalWifiAdapter`) MUST explicitly specify `sync: false` to prevent re-entrant deadlocks during mutex lock acquisition.
6. **A11y:** 48x48dp touch targets minimum, expand abbreviations in `Semantics` labels, support `TextScaler.linear(2.0)`.
7. **No Product Identity & Compliance Invariant Suite:** Strictly SRD 5.1 & 5.2 open content (CC-BY-4.0). Tests and code must use generic or invented homebrew names. Static bundled compendiums (`SrdClassesLibrary.allOptions`, `SrdBackgroundsLibrary.allBackgrounds`, `SrdSpeciesLibrary.allSpecies`, `MonsterCodexLibrary.allMonsters`, `MagicItemLibrary.allItems`) are verified via `srd_legal_compliance_test.dart` to contain zero proprietary Wizards of the Coast Product Identity tokens.
8. **Compendium Deduplication:** SrdEquivalenceIndex uses base SRD collections (`srdSpells`, `baseClasses`, etc.) unpolluted by homebrew; batch imports filter SRD canon by default (`excludeSrdCanon: true`); re-parse updates both Hive and SharedPreferences.
9. **Continuous Documentation & Living Rules Protocol:** Before completing any run, sync [README.md](file:///README.md) (features, tests, architecture) and audit/codify newly learned patterns or corrections into [AGENTS.md](file:///AGENTS.md), [GEMINI.md](file:///GEMINI.md), and [.agents/rules/](file:///.agents/rules/).
10. **System-Wide Backup Parity:** All backup services (`DmBackupService`, `AppBackupService`, `HomebrewPersistenceService`) must export and restore all 10 homebrew categories (`spells`, `monsters`, `items`, `classes`, `subclasses`, `races`, `subraces`, `feats`, `backgrounds`, `otherEntries`), reconciling standalone and orphan subraces without data loss.
11. **Combat Action Rider AST & Zero Runtime Regex:** Attack action descriptions are parsed at the ingestion boundary (`StatBlockAclParser.extractRiders`) into strongly-typed `CombatEffectRider` ASTs. During combat simulation (`ArenaCombatant.applyAttackHit`), attacks execute composite riders (`ConditionRider`, `AttributeDrainRider`, `MaxHpReductionRider`, `ForcedMovementRider`, `HealingSupressionRider`, `PeriodicDamageRider`) with zero runtime regex, clamping `effectiveMaxHp` and dynamically scaling down attack and saving throw modifiers.
12. **Subrace Character Builder Integration & Retention:** Character drafts track `subraceRef` (`EntityReference`), validated in `CharacterBuilderScreen` and resolved through `SkillTraitResolver.getSpeciesTraits(subraceSlug: draft.subraceRef?.slug)`. Subraces attached to SRD canon are indexed in `SrdSpeciesLibrary.customSubraces` and gathered during bundle exports to ensure zero loss of species lineages. Subrace selection UI wraps list items in `Material(color: ..., shape: RoundedRectangleBorder(...))` (without conflicting `borderRadius`) to prevent Flutter background assertions.
13. **Universal Item Codex & Character Sheet Cross-Loading:** `MagicItemLibrary.allItems` serves as the centralized source of truth synchronized from `HomebrewPersistenceService.syncToLibraries()` (`equipmentItemToMagicItem`). Homebrew items are browsable in `ItemCompendiumScreen`, assignable to any character roster sheet directly via `ItemDetailDialog`, addable within `CharacterSheetController.addItem()`, and selectable in the Character Builder equipment dialog.
14. **Universal Fluff & Lore Ingestion Pipeline:** Community compendium fluff bundles (including `monsterFluff`, `spellFluff`, `itemFluff`, `raceFluff`, `classFluff`, `subclassFluff`, etc.) and standalone fluff entries are recognized without artificial schema markers. Pure fluff maps infer entity categories from attributes (`className` -> `subclass`), image paths (`bestiary/` -> `monster`), or codex libraries. Two-pass deferred resolution resolves `_copy` references so variant lore and artwork are fully inherited. `IngestionBatchResult` and `HomebrewBundle` carry `List<EntityFluff> fluff`, seamlessly bridging background compute isolate boundaries into `EntityFluffService` and remote repository ingestion.
15. **SRD Index Decoupling & Fluff/Subrace Persistence Parity:** `SrdEquivalenceIndex` indexes only immutable `SrdEquipmentLibrary.baseEquipmentItems`, preventing custom homebrew equipment from being flagged as SRD canon and purged during reparse operations. `HomebrewPersistenceService` persists and exports `subraces` and `fluff` alongside primary entity registries, routing custom parent races when non-SRD species are referenced. Per-item `try-catch` deserialization ensures corrupted or malformed payloads do not discard entire compendium categories.
16. **Rolling vs Data Table Differentiation & Tabular UI Rendering:** Compendium tables are classified into `HomebrewOtherCategory.tables` (Rollable Tables) vs `HomebrewOtherCategory.dataTables` (Data & Reference Tables) via `isRollingTable`. Both types sync to `DmScreenLibrary` and render in `RulesCompendiumScreen` with subcategory filter chips (`All Tables`, `Rollable Tables`, `Data & Reference Tables`). Markdown tables (`| ... |`) inside rule cards (`DmRuleCard`) are grouped and rendered as native Flutter `Table` widgets via `FormattedMarkdownText` instead of unstructured bullet points. To prevent horizontal `RenderFlex` overflow under narrow viewports or 2.0x dynamic type scaling, card badges (`Rollable`, `Data Table`, `Homebrew`, `EditionDiffBadge`) are placed in the subtitle `Wrap` inside an `Expanded` column rather than placed as unconstrained siblings in the top-level card `Row`.
17. **Monster Feature ACL Discrepancy Resolution & Non-Tabletop Filtering:** Non-tabletop crochet and recipe assets (`recipes.json`, `recipe`, `recipes`, `recipefluff`) are excluded across remote manifest discovery and unpacking. Monster features, actions, and combat traits evaluated during ingestion (`MonsterCombatProfile.fromStatBlock`, `CompendiumGenericEntryParser`) are validated against the internal ACL parser (`StatBlockAclParser.parseMonsterFeature` and `StatBlockAclParser.parseStatBlockBoundary`). Any discrepancy in mobility (`canFly`, `canSwim`, `canClimb`, `canBurrow`, `hasHover`), reach, saves, spell save DC, spell attack bonuses, or action riders strictly favors the internal ACL parser over raw incoming statblock declarations.
18. **CvRDT Ledger Batching & Import Performance:** `CrdtOrSet.addBatch` replaces single-item additions to eliminate $O(N^2)$ map re-allocations during compendium streaming. `HomebrewImportPreviewDialog` pre-buckets compendium categories once during analysis, restricts `initiallyExpanded` to lists with `<= 50` items to eliminate initial widget tree explosion, and throttles `onProgress` `setState` updates to at most once per 50 items or 80ms. `SrdEquivalenceIndex.build()` checks `isBuilt` to prevent redundant index rebuilds on every chunk of 50 entities.
19. **Markdown Table Parsing & Detail Dialog Parity:** `FormattedMarkdownText` isolates markdown tables from touching paragraphs via `_isolateMarkdownTables`, normalizes uneven rows to `maxCols` to prevent fatal Flutter `Table` assertion errors, preserves escaped pipes `\|` within cells, and applies zebra-striped styling with horizontal scroll. Detail and comparison views (`DmRuleComparisonDialog._buildRuleWidgets`) group consecutive `|` lines and delegate to `FormattedMarkdownText` instead of prepending raw pipe rows with bullet points.
20. **Universal Compendium & Codex Markdown Formatting:** All compendium cards and detail dialogs (`ItemCard`, `ItemDetailDialog`, `SpellCard`, `SpellComparisonDialog`, `CreatureStatBlockDialog`, `FeatCard`, `HomebrewStudioScreen` preview dialog, `CharacterBuilderScreen`, and `AddFeatDialog`) MUST use `FormattedMarkdownText` to render formatted descriptions and embedded markdown tables rather than plain Flutter `Text`. When rendering line-capped previews on card surfaces, `FormattedMarkdownText` supports `maxLines` and `overflow` (`TextOverflow.ellipsis`) to cleanly display bold/italic inline text and handle multi-block previews.
21. **Subrace Attribute Replacement, Skill, & Spell Ingestion, & Markdown Formatting:** `Subrace` preserves all mechanical attributes (`abilityScoreSummary`, `fixedAbilityBonuses`, `flexibleAbilityCount`, `flexibleAbilityBonus`, `speed`, `darkvision`, and `grants`). `CompendiumRaceParser.parseSubrace` normalizes ability keys to standard lowercase names (`dexterity`, `intelligence`, etc.) and extracts skills, tools, and bonus spells from `skillProficiencies`, `additionalSpells`, and `trait`/`entries`. In 2014 mode, subrace attributes REPLACE base species attributes rather than adding to them: when a subrace specifies ability score increases (e.g. High Elf `+2 DEX, +1 INT`, Mountain Dwarf `+2 CON, +2 STR`, or variant lineage subraces), walking speed (e.g. Wood Elf 35 ft), or darkvision (e.g. Drow 120 ft), `CharacterBuilderScreen` and `AbilityScoreStep` replace the base species values with the subrace's values. Innate cantrips and spells from subraces/species (`SkillTraitResolver.getInnateSpeciesSpells`) are auto-registered upon character finalization, and subrace skill proficiencies are passed to `SkillTraitResolver.resolveSkills` with collision detection. All subrace descriptions across `CharacterBuilderScreen`, `RaceCard`, and `RaceDetailDialog` MUST be rendered using `FormattedMarkdownText` with structured attribute badges (`+2 DEX, +1 INT`, `Speed 35 ft.`, `Darkvision 120 ft.`) rather than plain, regex-stripped text.
22. **Static Compendium Legal Compliance & Test Sanitization:** `srd_legal_compliance_test.dart` enforces that all bundled collections (`SrdClassesLibrary.allOptions`, `SrdBackgroundsLibrary.allBackgrounds`, `SrdSpeciesLibrary.allSpecies`, `MonsterCodexLibrary.allMonsters`, `MagicItemLibrary.allItems`) contain zero proprietary Product Identity terms (such as non-SRD campaign settings, trademarked monsters, named wizards, or setting-specific character lineages). Test fixtures must strictly use generic variant lineages (`Human (Lineage of Artifice)`, `CUSTOM_LINEAGE`) instead of proprietary settings or marks.
23. **Action Rider Execution, Subrace Pool Constraints, & Background Compilation:** Attack actions and spells extract condition saving throw riders (`ConditionRider`, knockdown prone) with dynamic fallback DC calculation ($8 + \text{PB} + \text{AbilityMod}$). `ArenaCombatant.applyAttackHit` executes saving throw checks, manages HP via domain `HitPoints`, and appends formatted pass/fail condition logs to `ArenaAttackEvent.summaryText`. Subrace flexible choices are bounded by `flexibleAbilityPool`, preventing unselected choices from defaulting to Strength in 2014 mode. Background skills, starting equipment instances, and 2024 ASIs are normalized and granted onto compiled characters by `CharacterFactory.buildFromDraft()`.
24. **WebRTC Async Microtask Dispatch & Turn Simulation Rider Telemetry:** `WebRtcMeshAdapter` initializes `_incomingPayloadsController` with `broadcast(sync: false)` and schedules DataChannel message emission via `scheduleMicrotask()` to eliminate event-loop contention and prevent re-entrant mutex deadlocks. `ArenaCombatEngine._resolveSingleAttack` looks up `PrecomputedAttack` from monster combat profiles, delegates execution to `defender.applyAttackHit`, and forwards `hitResult.riderLogs` directly into `ArenaAttackEvent.appliedRiderLogs` to seamlessly format and append action rider telemetry to `summaryText`.
25. **Shadow Strength Drain & Instant Death Hardening:** `StatBlockAclParser.extractRiders` parses `AttributeDrainRider` with `deathAtZero: true` on regex variations of `dies if this reduces (?:its\s+)?(?:Strength|ability|score)? to 0`. In `ArenaCombatant`, reducing Strength or effectiveMaxHp to 0 sets `hitPoints = hitPoints.copyWith(currentHp: 0)`, logs `"$displayName's ${targetAbility.shortName.toUpperCase()} was reduced to 0! $displayName dies instantly!"`, and `isAlive` immediately returns `false` (and `isDefeated` returns `true`) whenever `getAbilityScore(AbilityType.strength) <= 0`.
26. **Subrace Fixed & Flexible ASI Compilation:** In 2014 mode, `CharacterDraft.reconcile()` reconciles `pendingFlexibleAbilityChoices` strictly against `subraceRef.flexibleAbilityPool` without defaulting to Strength, and computes `speciesBonusScores` (base species fixed bonuses + subrace fixed bonuses + selected flexible choices), enforcing `backgroundBonusScores = AbilityScores.zero()`. `CharacterValidationEngine.reconcileDraft` preserves `bonusScores = updated.speciesBonusScores` instead of zeroing it.
27. **2014 Background Ingestion & Starting Equipment/Feature Injection:** `SrdBackgroundsLibrary.findBySlug` returns hydrated `Background` instances with canonical 2014 skill proficiencies, starting equipment items (`extractStartingEquipment`), and narrative background features (`get2014Feature`, e.g., 'Shelter of the Faithful' for Acolyte). During `CharacterFactory.buildFromDraft`, 2014 backgrounds grant skill proficiencies, instantiate starting equipment into `character.inventory` (unless `draft.takesStartingWealth` is true), and inject narrative background features into `character.customProperties['backgroundFeature']` without polluting `character.feats` or generating background ASIs.
28. **Lineage Spell Grant Extraction & Artificer Starting Tool Proficiencies:** `FeatureGrant.extractBonusSpells` and `CompendiumRaceParser._extractGrants` comprehensively extract spells from `additionalSpells` across `innate` (including nested `daily` maps), `prepared`, `known`, and `expanded` (spell slot mapping `s1`..`s5`), detecting cantrip flags (`#c`) to populate `FeatureGrant.bonusSpell(isCantrip: true)`. `SkillTraitResolver.getInnateSpeciesSpells` resolves prefix-stripped subrace slugs, differentiates character level (`innate`, `known`, `prepared`) from spell slot level (`expanded`), and `CharacterFactory.buildFromDraft` auto-merges species/subrace cantrips into `character.cantrips` and innate/expanded spells into `character.spellsKnown`. `SkillTraitResolver.resolveTools` guarantees Artificer starting tool proficiencies (`Thieves' Tools`, `Tinker's Tools`, and artisan tools) and background tools during `CharacterFactory.buildFromDraft` and `CharacterDraft.reconcile`. In `CharacterBuilderScreen._buildClassToolsPrompt`, an Artificer prompt allows selecting an artisan's tool choice while displaying granted tools, and `_buildStepSpells` includes expanded species spells in class spell choices.
29. **Storage Durability & Eviction-Immunity Architecture:** Pure domain models in `lib/domain/storage/models/` (`EngineProfile`, `StorageTelemetryReport`, `StorageSnapshotBundle`) and port contracts (`IStorageDurabilityPort`, `IPhysicalSnapshotPort`) must maintain zero Flutter dependencies. `EngineProfile` classifies browser engines and OS using deterministic token substring matching without regular expressions, accurately identifying WebKit 7-day eviction risks (`isWebKitEvictionRisk`) and Firefox gesture fences (`requiresExplicitGesture`). `StorageDurabilityCoordinator.executeSilentPreflight()` runs prior to IndexedDB/Hive connection pool initialization to prevent browsers from locking storage into ephemeral tiers, suppressing boot prompts on Firefox Desktop while silently requesting persistence on Chromium and WebKit Standalone PWAs. `StorageSnapshotBundle` seals cold-storage backups with pure Dart constant-time SHA-256 verification against `SHA-256(vaultId + payloadBytes)`. Hydration validates timestamps against a 30-second sliding lookback window to prevent stale snapshots from overwriting fresh local CRDT vectors. `StorageDurabilityBanner` enforces a zero-friction invariant (hidden when persisted or standalone PWA), displaying non-modal dismissible warnings for Safari tabs and high-contrast alerts for storage pressure $\ge 80\%$ with WCAG 48x48dp touch targets and 2.0x Dynamic Type scaling. PWA app shell cache shield (`web/service_worker.js` and `web/sw.js`) applies immutable cache-first caching to app shell assets (`index.html`, `flutter.js`, `main.dart.js`, `canvaskit/*`, wasm binaries) while strictly excluding APIs and dynamic websocket streams to preserve IndexedDB authority.
30. **Compendium Class Starting Skills & Attributes-First Flexible Lineage Architecture:** Ingest class starting skill options (`startingProficiencies.skills`, `choose.from`, `any`, lists, `{@skill}` tags) via `CompendiumClassParser.parseClassSkills` into `CharacterClass.allowedSkills` and `skillChoiceCount`. `CharacterDraft.reconcile()` reconciles `pendingFlexibleAbilityChoices` into `speciesBonusScores` for base species as well as subraces. In `CharacterBuilderScreen`, when stats precede race (e.g. `attributesFirst`), selecting a flexible lineage (Half-Elf, Variant Human, Custom Lineage) prompts for choices inline inside the card with live attribute totals and gates progression until selected, while standard ordering allows choosing during species or ability scores steps.
31. **Class Skill Choice Pool Ingestion, Signaling Path Consistency, & DPR Rider Simulation:** `HomebrewEntityDto._detectEntityType` recognizes classes by signature keys (`hitDie`, `classFeatures`, `startingProficiencies`). `_normalizeAndClamp` parses nested `startingProficiencies.skills`, `proficiencyChoices`, natural language choices ("Choose 2 from..."), and flat skills arrays into `normalizedData['skillProficiencies']` and `normalizedData['flexibleSkills']`, while adding all class keys to `recognizedKeys` to prevent unparsed payload leakage. `HomebrewIngestor.mapClassFromDto` maps DTOs into domain `CharacterClass` / `ClassDefinition`. `FirebaseSignalingAdapter._sendSignal` defers recording tracked paths in `_peerTrackedDocPaths` until Firestore `doc().set()` succeeds cleanly, preventing phantom document paths on failed network writes. `DprSimulator.run()` evaluates ongoing damage and numeric attribute drain from `PeriodicDamageRider` and `AttributeDrainRider` on landed hits without intermediate heap allocations, accurately reflecting rider contributions in mean damage and DPR. `_DangerouslyNerdy5eToolkitAppState.didChangeAppLifecycleState` safely coordinates async storage flushes via error-isolated `flushAll().catchError(...)` to guard against unhandled rejections during background termination.
32. **Field-Level Sub-Resource Reconciliation, Bi-Directional Step-Up Recovery, & Polite Glare Rollback:** Rather than whole-document LWW overwrite of `CampaignProfile` upon receiving `room_sync_full` payloads, state convergence via `RoomStateReconciliationService.reconcileProfile` merges sub-resources independently (notes, party purse, party rosters, active minions keyed by `m.id`, active encounters keyed by `participantId`, room metadata, and change logs). `RoomSyncOrchestrator` stamps outbound frames with monotonic sequence numbers and originating node IDs, suppressing self-echo loops and remote sync echo broadcasts. `CascadingTransportRouter` monitors health and probes higher-tier adapters (`probeHigherTiers()`) via the abstract `IP2pTransportPort.probeViability()` contract to step up from fallback cloud back to WebRTC without downcasting or leaking concrete adapter implementations. `WebRtcMeshAdapter` implements W3C polite peer glare rollback (`RTCSessionDescription('', 'rollback')`) to cleanly resolve simultaneous offer collisions without tearing down peer connections. `RoomStateReconciliationService.safePrune` evaluates pruning thresholds against monotonic network-synchronized physical time (`INetworkTimePort`), deferring pruning gracefully when local clock skew would invert pruning horizons ($threshold \ge networkTime$). Canonical `RulesetEdition` serves as the pure domain enumeration with bi-directional adapters, and `StorageChecksum` verifies storage payload integrity using `package:crypto`.
34. **Clean Domain Boundaries & Read-Only Repository Invariant:** `CampaignProfile` and `CampaignProfileDto` are strictly decoupled from mutable character migration baggage (`_migratedCharacters` eradicated). Legacy character extraction is handled explicitly via `CampaignProfileDto.extractLegacyCharacters()` at the service boundary. `LocalCampaignRepository.loadAllProfiles()` is strictly read-only and predictable, devoid of character repository write side-effects or synthetic room membership generation.
35. **PN-Counter Currency Convergence, Transport Flapping Dampening, & 5e RAW 0 HP / Healing Semantics:**
    - Party currency denominations (`cp, sp, ep, gp, pp`) in `PartyPurse` converge using CvRDT `PnCounter` (positive/negative increments per nodeId), preventing spent currency from being resurrected by stale remote balances.
    - Milestone pruning (`safePrune` / `executeMilestonePrune`) safely defers when `epoch >= currentNetworkTime`, preventing premature tombstone purges during clock skew.
    - `RoomSyncOrchestrator` replaces the coarse persistence mutex with mutation tokens and an in-memory SHA-256 hash ring (`_appliedProfileHashRing`), eliminating deadlock hazards.
    - `CascadingTransportRouter` enforces sequential failure thresholds (`sequentialFailureThreshold = 3`) and holdoff cooldowns (`stepUpHoldoff = 5s`) to dampen transport flapping.
    - `FirebaseSignalingAdapter` tracks wildcard and authored signaling documents in `_authoredDocPaths`, purging all documents on session teardown and author-only documents on peer disconnects.
    - `UserAgentParser` decouples browser sniffing from `EngineProfile` into infrastructure, and `StorageDurabilityCoordinator.hydrateFromColdStorage` permits user-initiated `.dndvault` rollbacks validated by constant-time SHA-256.
    - `HitPoints` strictly decouples 0 HP unconsciousness (`isDowned`) from permanent death (`isDead`). Standard healing (`heal(amount)`) revives and heals downed creatures (0 HP) per 5e RAW, while permanent death from massive damage (excess damage >= maxHp) requires explicit revival (`allowRevive: true` or `.revive()`).
36. **Transport State Domain Decoupling, crdt_purse_delta Protocol, & DataChannel Signaling Gate:**
    - `TransportState` is a pure domain contract residing in `lib/domain/ports/transport_state.dart`. `IP2pTransportPort` has zero application imports, strictly upholding the Dependency Inversion Principle. Ports in `lib/domain/ports/` must never import infrastructure DTOs.
    - Party currency mutations (`PartyPurse.modifyCoin(denomination, delta, nodeId: id)`) dynamically route positive/negative adjustments to CvRDT `PnCounter` using UUID v4 node identifiers.
    - `RoomStateReconciliationService._mergePartyPurse` unconditionally joins coin counters (`local.merge(remote)`), completely eradicating scalar Last-Write-Wins overwrites.
    - `RoomSyncOrchestrator` monitors party purse changes and emits focused `crdt_purse_delta` payloads over transport meshes while reconciling inbound purse deltas independently, eliminating full document dumps on routine currency operations.
    - In `WebRtcMeshAdapter`, ICE connection state transitions to `connected` defer ephemeral signaling document deletion until the peer DataChannel is fully established (`RTCDataChannelState.RTCDataChannelOpen`) or ICE state reaches `completed`, preventing premature handshake cleanup.
37. **Vectorized Reconnection Ordering, Pure CRDT Reconciliation, Dedicated Mailboxes & Lifecycle Flushes:**
    - `RoomSyncOrchestrator` replaces strict sequence drop checks (`originSeq <= lastSeq`) with per-node sequence vector clocks (`_nodeSequenceVectors`) and offline edit tracking. Out-of-order relays and offline burst mutations merge deterministically into state without dropping valid mutations, while maintaining 30s sliding lookback and 500-entry LRU deduplication. Envelope serialization is decoupled into `RoomSyncPayloadMapper`.
    - `RoomStateReconciliationService` eliminates all scalar wall-clock comparisons (`inboundTimestamp >= localTimestamp`). Minions and encounter participants reconcile via `CrdtOrSet` with HLC tombstones, campaign notes converge via `CrdtLwwRegister<String>`, and party purse converges via `PnCounter`. Generative `const RoomNodeState` is preserved with rich collection ergonomics, alongside `RoomNodeState.fromLists` for dynamic iterables.
    - `FirebaseSignalingAdapter` isolates P2P signaling handshakes into dedicated recipient mailboxes (`rooms/{roomCode}/nodes/{toNodeId}/signals/{signalId}`), eliminating wildcard race conditions, cross-peer signal collisions, and premature document deletion in multi-peer topologies. `LocalWifiAdapter.probeViability` implements non-destructive UDP probing with socket re-use to safely determine Tier 1 step-up viability without crashing bound sockets.
    - `LocalCampaignRepository._persistProfileToDisk` writes campaign profile data and index keys atomically via `AppDatabaseService.putAll`, preventing index desynchronization on unexpected termination. `_DangerouslyNerdy5eToolkitAppState` wires `AppLifecycleListener` for awaited asynchronous storage flushes (`onPause`, `onHide`, `onDetach`, `onExitRequested`) with error isolation.
38. **Stateless Room Stub Rehydration & 30-Day Lease Auto-Renewal:**
    - In stateless P2P / WebRTC mesh rooms, Firestore subcollections (`nodes/{toNodeId}/signals` and `relay_messages`) exist without requiring a static parent `/rooms/{roomCode}` document.
    - When a player connects via `PartyRoomService.joinCampaign()`, if the root `/rooms/{code}` document is missing, the service verifies active presence in `rooms/{code}/nodes` and `rooms/{code}/relay_messages` before concluding the room does not exist, preventing false `"Campaign not found"` errors.
    - Whenever *anyone* connects (host room initialization, player join, or `PartyRoomScreen` mount), `PartyRoomService.ensureRoomExists`, `FirebaseSignalingAdapter.initialize`, and `FirebaseFallbackAdapter.initializeRoom` touch/rehydrate `/rooms/{cleanCode}` with `isStateless: true` and reset the 30-day lease (`expiresAt: now + 30 days`, `lastUpdated: now`) using `SetOptions(merge: true)`.
    - Non-existent rooms lacking active signaling nodes, relay messages, or local DM credentials continue to throw `CampaignNotFoundException` without creating ghost documents.
    - Firestore security rules (`firestore.rules`) permit `partyPurse` PN-counter keys (`cpCounter`, `spCounter`, `epCounter`, `gpCounter`, `ppCounter`) and stateless room lease renewals without permission errors.
39. **2014 Background Import Parity & Outbox Wedge-Immunity:**
    - In 2014 rules, backgrounds do not grant Ability Score Increases (ASIs). `HomebrewEntityDto._validateSrd2014Contract` permits 2014 background JSON from GitHub or community compendiums without `ability`/`asi` fields or with empty/null ability arrays. ASI stripping and validation are strictly handled downstream during character compilation in `CharacterFactory.buildFromDraft` and `CharacterValidationEngine`.
    - All room currency and roster operations (`depositCoins`, `withdrawCoins`, `setActiveCharacter`, `addCharacterToRoster`) use `docRef.set(..., SetOptions(merge: true))` with `roomCode`, `code`, and `campaignName`, preventing Firestore `NOT_FOUND` errors on fresh stateless stubs.
    - `PartyRoomService.flushOutbox` aggregates all pending coin operations across the outbox batch into a single document write to avoid Firestore batch collision exceptions, increments `retryCount` on `PartyOutboxAction`, and purges poison pills after 3 consecutive failures to prevent infinite `Syncing (N)` UI spinning. `clearOutbox(roomCode)` enables immediate outbox resets.
40. **Firestore Security Rules Claim Parity, Auto-Sync & Campaign Lifecycle Management:**
    - **Host Key Hash Claim on Existing Stubs:** In `firestore.rules`, room updates permit DMs to set `hostKeyHash` when transitioning an untracked or stateless stub into an authorized DM room: `(!('hostKeyHash' in resource.data) || resource.data.hostKeyHash == '' || !('hostKeyHash' in request.resource.data) || request.resource.data.hostKeyHash == resource.data.hostKeyHash)`. Partial updates with matching `roomCode` or `code` succeed without strict single-field collisions.
    - **Firestore Deletion Permissions:** `firestore.rules` grants deletion on room documents and all subcollections when verified by App Check (`allow delete: if isAppCheckVerified();`), enabling full campaign cleanup.
    - **Clock Skew Tolerance:** Firestore timestamp validation tolerates $\pm 1$ hour of device drift: `request.time - duration.value(1, 'h') < ... && ... < request.time + duration.value(1, 'h')`.
    - **Automatic Existing Campaign Cloud Sync:** `PartyRoomService.syncAllExistingCampaignsToFirestore()` iterates all local campaign memberships on `LandingScreen` mount and rehydrates/ensures room stubs on Cloud Firestore with valid codes, campaign names, host key hashes, and party purses.

43. **Party Coin Synchronization, Scalar Priority, & Vault Zero-Wipe Protection:**
    - **Scalar Priority & Re-seeding:** `PartyPurse.fromMap` prioritizes scalar coin denominations (`cp`, `sp`, `ep`, `gp`, `pp`) over stale PN-counters whenever scalars are present and differ from counter values (e.g. after atomic `FieldValue.increment` writes in Firestore). 45. **Vault Fund Dispersal vs External Loot & CvRDT Lattice Join Idempotence:**
    - **Vault Dispersal Zero-Sum Reallocation:** Dispersing coins from the shared Party Vault reserve (`isVaultDispersal: true` in `PartyRoomService.disperseCoinsToParty` and `DisperseLootDialog.show`) MUST withdraw character shares from `partyPurse` via `withdrawCoins(...)`, strictly conserving total party wealth without monetary inflation. Dispersing external loot hoards (`isVaultDispersal: false`) deposits reserve shares and remainders as newly discovered treasure.
    - **CvRDT Join Idempotence:** In `PartyPurse.fromMap`, scalar adjustments differing from PN-counters apply deterministic incremental deltas on `nodeId: 'cloud'` rather than overwriting the counter or inventing uncoordinated node IDs, guaranteeing CvRDT lattice join idempotence ($A \sqcup A = A$) and preventing exponential balance compounding across sync intervals.
46. **Hexagonal Boundary Inversion, Clock Skew Immunization, Mutex Narrowing & Simulation Determinism:**
    - **Decoupled Snapshot Serialization & Inbound Room Payload Ports:** Application services (`StorageDurabilityCoordinator`, `RoomSyncOrchestrator`) must never depend on infrastructure DTOs (`CampaignProfileDto`, `CharacterTelemetryDto`). Snapshot serialization is inverted via `ICampaignSnapshotSerializerPort` (`lib/domain/storage/ports/`), implemented by `CampaignSnapshotSerializerAdapter` (`lib/infrastructure/adapters/storage/`) using `Isolate.run()`.     - **Deterministic Monte Carlo Simulation Seeding:** `DprSimulator.runInIsolate` accepts an optional `int? seed` parameter, forwarded to isolate worker configurations and initializing `Random(seed)` for bit-identical, reproducible combat simulations across benchmark runs and regression suites.
47. **CvRDT PN-Counter Reduction Invariant, Full Character Storage, & Local Persistence Priority:**
    - **Differential Decrement Enforcement:** In `PartyPurse`, all reductions to coin balances (via `setCoins`, `modifyCoin`, or `copyWith`) calculate the delta against `effectiveCounter` and record negative decrements (`counter.decrement(nodeId, delta)`) rather than re-seeding the counter with a positive scalar via `PnCounter.withInitialValue(val)`. Re-seeding leaves the negative register empty, transforming the PN-counter into a monotonically grow-only maximum register across CvRDT lattice joins (`max(pos1, pos2)`).
    - **Full Character Storage in Party Rooms:** In `PartyRoomService`, `session.sharedCharacters` stores the full `character.toMap()` payload rather than truncated `CharacterTelemetryDto.toMap()`, preventing `CharacterResourcePool` from resetting to 10 HP. `Character.toMap()` explicitly includes `maxHp`, `currentHp`, `tempHp`, and `armorClass`, while `Character.fromMap()` and `CharacterResourcePool.fromMap()` defensively fall back to top-level `hp`/`currentHp` and `thp`/`tempHp`.
    - **Local Persistence Priority & Auto-Sync:** UI consumers opening character sheets in party rooms check local persistent storage (`CharacterPersistenceService.getCharacter`) first before remote `session.sharedCharacters`, and push updated telemetry and member purses back to the party room on route return. `LocalCharacterRepository` maintains zero unsynchronized in-memory caches, persisting immediately and synchronously mirroring to both Hive and `SharedPreferences` for complete read-after-write consistency across services.
